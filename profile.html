ℹ️ About CivicEye AI

CivicEye AI is a citizen-focused platform designed to make reporting and tracking public issues easier.

The platform supports AI-assisted issue identification, location tracking, status updates, notifications and analytics.

Built with the goal of helping create cleaner, safer and better communities in Sri Lanka 🇱🇰
