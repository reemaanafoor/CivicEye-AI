ℹ️ About CivicEye AI

CivicEye AI is an AI-powered platform designed to help citizens report and track public issues more easily.

The system can identify issue types, detect duplicate complaints, recommend the relevant authority and provide status updates.

🇱🇰 Built with the goal of creating cleaner, safer and smarter communities.
