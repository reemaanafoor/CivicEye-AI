Help & Support

Find helpful information about using CivicEye AI and its main features.

How to Report an Issue

Open the Report page, select the issue type, provide the location and description, and submit your complaint.

AI Issue Analysis

CivicEye AI analyzes reported issues and helps identify the issue category, priority and recommended authority.

Duplicate Complaint Detection

CivicEye AI checks whether a similar complaint has already been submitted for the same type of issue and location.

Notifications

Citizens can receive notifications about their complaints and important complaint updates.

Track Your Complaint

Use the Track section to follow the current status and progress of a submitted complaint.

Need More Help?

Use the available CivicEye AI features to report, monitor and manage civic issues through the platform.
