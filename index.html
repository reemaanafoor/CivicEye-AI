CivicEye AI

CivicEye AI is an intelligent public issue reporting and management platform designed to connect citizens and authorities. The system uses AI-assisted analysis to help identify and categorize common public problems such as road damage, garbage, water leaks, and street light failures. Citizens can submit an issue using an image, description, and location information. The submitted information can then be processed and monitored through the CivicEye system. CivicEye AI aims to demonstrate how technology can support cleaner, safer, and smarter communities across Sri Lanka.

🚀 How CivicEye AI Works

A simple digital process connects citizens with issue management tools.

1️⃣ Report

Citizens upload an image and provide information about a public issue.

2️⃣ AI Analysis

AI-assisted analysis can help identify and categorize the reported issue.

3️⃣ Location

Location information can help identify where the public issue was reported.

4️⃣ Monitor

Authorities can review reports and update their progress through the dashboard.

🚀 Our Smart Features

🤖

AI Issue Detection

AI-assisted image analysis can identify and categorize common public issues such as road damage and garbage.

📍

Smart Location Tracking

Location information can help identify where a public issue has been reported.

🔍

Duplicate Complaint Detection

Similar reports can be identified to help reduce repeated complaints about the same issue.

🚨

Emergency Priority

Reported issues can be assigned a priority level to help identify cases that may need faster attention.

🏛️

Authority Recommendation

The system can recommend a relevant local authority based on the issue category and location.

🔄

Complaint Status Tracking

Citizens can follow the progress of their submitted complaints through the CivicEye system.

✅

Resolution Verification

Citizens can be given an opportunity to verify whether a reported issue has been resolved.

🎙️

Tri-Language Voice Complaints

Voice input can support English, Tamil, and Sinhala when available through the system.
