About CivicEye AI

CivicEye AI is an AI-powered civic issue reporting web application designed to help citizens report public issues in a simple and organized way.

What CivicEye AI Does

The platform combines complaint reporting, AI-assisted issue analysis, duplicate detection, priority assessment, authority recommendation and complaint tracking.

Key Features

AI Issue Analysis

Helps analyze submitted civic issues and identify their category.

Duplicate Detection

Identifies possible repeated complaints based on the issue and reported location.

Priority Detection

Helps identify the urgency of reported civic issues.

Authority Recommendation

Suggests the relevant authority for handling the reported issue.

Citizen Notifications

Keeps citizens informed about important complaint events and updates.

Complaint Tracking

Allows citizens to follow the progress of their submitted complaints.
