🔐 Privacy & Data Protection

CivicEye AI respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

👤 Account Information

CivicEye AI may store basic account information such as your email address to provide citizen account features and access your reports.

📋 Complaint Data

Information submitted through a public issue report may include the issue type, description, uploaded image, location and report status. This information is used to process and track public complaints.

📍 Location Information

If you choose to provide your location, CivicEye AI may use the location information to help identify where the reported public issue occurred.

📷 Uploaded Images

Images uploaded with a complaint are used to analyse and document the reported issue.

🔔 Notifications

CivicEye AI may send notifications about report status changes and other relevant updates.

🗑️ Data Retention

Solved reports may be automatically removed after the configured retention period.

🛡️ Data Protection

CivicEye AI is designed to use collected information only for providing and improving the public issue reporting and tracking service.
