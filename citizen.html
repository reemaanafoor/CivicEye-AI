ℹ️ About CivicEye AI

CivicEye AI is an AI-assisted platform designed to help citizens report and track public issues more easily.

The system can identify reported issues, recommend relevant authorities, detect possible duplicate complaints and provide status notifications.

Built to support smarter and more responsive communities in Sri Lanka. 🇱🇰
